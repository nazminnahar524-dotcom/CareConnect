Prepare for Your Visit

Helpful steps before your consultation

Arrive Early

Aim to arrive 15–20 minutes before your scheduled appointment. This allows time for registration, check-in and locating the doctor's chamber.

Bring Medical Reports

Bring relevant prescriptions, previous test reports, diagnostic results and a list of medicines you currently take.

Confirm the Hospital Chamber

Verify the chamber location, visiting hours and appointment confirmation with the hospital before travelling.

Prepare Your Questions

Make a note of your symptoms, when they started, relevant medical history and questions you want to discuss with the doctor.
